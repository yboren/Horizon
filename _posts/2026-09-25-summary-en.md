## [OpenAI Launches MentalHealthBench for AI Mental Health Evaluation](https://openai.com/zh-Hans-CN/index/introducing-mentalhealthbench/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI has released MentalHealthBench, an open benchmark consisting of 1,215 realistic mental health conversations developed by over 80 licensed mental health professionals from 22 countries. It evaluates how AI models handle safety, background information collection, user autonomy, and actionable advice across various scenarios. This benchmark addresses a critical gap in AI safety by providing a standardized, expert-informed framework to assess LLMs in sensitive therapeutic contexts. It helps ensure that AI tools are developed responsibly while emphasizing that they cannot replace professional clinical treatment. The benchmark covers diverse user groups, including adults, adolescents, caregivers, and clinical staff, to ensure comprehensive testing. While results show steady progress in AI capabilities, the benchmark explicitly warns against using ChatGPT as a substitute for professional therapy.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 24, 06:00

**Background**: Evaluating LLMs in mental health is notoriously difficult due to the complex, emotional, and high-stakes nature of therapeutic dialogue. Existing benchmarks often rely on synthetic data or social media posts, which lack the clinical rigor required for real-world support. MentalHealthBench aims to improve reliability by grounding evaluations in established principles from clinical psychology.
