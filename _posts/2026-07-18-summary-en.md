## [First atmosphere detected on a rocky, habitable-zone exoplanet](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cy4kdd1e0ejo) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Astronomers have utilized JWST data to identify a nitrogen-rich atmosphere on LHS 1140b, a rocky exoplanet located 48 light-years away in the habitable zone of a red dwarf star. This discovery marks a major milestone in exoplanetary research, providing the first clear evidence of an atmosphere on a potentially habitable, rocky world and significantly advancing our search for life beyond the solar system. LHS 1140b is a super-Earth that orbits a red dwarf star; emission spectroscopy data from JWST has helped rule out the possibility that it is a mini-Neptune, confirming its rocky or ocean-world nature.

hackernews · neversaydie · Jul 17, 14:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48947560)

**Background**: LHS 1140b was discovered in 2017 and is classified as a super-Earth, meaning it is larger than Earth but smaller than Neptune. It orbits within the 'habitable zone' of its star, the region where temperatures are theoretically right for liquid water to exist on the surface. Red dwarfs are the most common stars in the galaxy, but they are often unstable, making the retention of an atmosphere on orbiting planets a significant scientific question.
