This was accomplished by meticulously matching PyTorch's specific, non-standard kernel implementations and compiler-fused operations. This project highlights the extreme difficulty of achieving deterministic results in deep learning, where subtle differences in hardware, compiler optimizations, and kernel implementations lead to divergent outcomes.
