## [Chinese Courts Rule Game Accounts and Virtual Assets Are Inheritable](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/big-tech/chinese-courts-allow-heirs-to-inherent-accounts-of-deceased-gamers-multiple-cases-spanning-years-establish-precedent-for-digital-ownership-of-games-in-game-items-and-microtransactions) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Chinese courts have established a legal precedent ruling that game accounts, virtual items, and digital assets are inheritable property. The rulings invalidate platform terms of service that prohibit the transfer of accounts after a user's death. This development challenges the 'license-only' model typically enforced by tech companies, asserting that users maintain property rights over their digital investments. It sets a significant precedent for digital inheritance and the legal status of virtual assets in the gaming industry. While virtual assets and accounts are inheritable, courts have clarified that personal content such as private chat logs remains excluded from inheritance to protect privacy. Platforms are required to facilitate the transfer process, though they may charge reasonable administrative fees.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jul 10, 02:56

**Background**: In the digital age, most gaming platforms operate under End User License Agreements (EULAs) that claim users only 'rent' their accounts rather than owning them. This legal framework has historically prevented heirs from claiming digital assets after a user passes away. These court rulings represent a shift toward recognizing digital property rights as equivalent to physical property under inheritance law.

**Discussion**: Discussions on platforms like Reddit reflect broad support for the ruling, with users arguing that since they spend significant money on virtual items, they should retain ownership rights. Some users expressed concerns about how platforms might implement the transfer process and whether it will lead to increased administrative hurdles.
