## [3D-Printed Bionic Corpus Cavernosum Offers New Therapy for Erectile Dysfunction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biomaterials.2026.124491) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers have developed a 3D-printed bionic corpus cavernosum seeded with umbilical cord-derived mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) that successfully restored erectile function in a porcine model. This breakthrough offers a potential regenerative medicine approach to treat erectile dysfunction at the structural level, moving beyond traditional symptom-management therapies. The MSCs facilitate tissue integration by promoting endothelial differentiation, inhibiting endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EndMT) via reduced TGF-β secretion, and modulating the immune environment through IL-10 activation.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 4, 13:52

**Background**: Erectile dysfunction (ED) is often caused by damage to the corpus cavernosum, the sponge-like tissue that fills with blood to cause an erection. Mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) are multipotent cells capable of differentiating into various cell types and modulating immune responses, making them a focus for regenerative therapies. Endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EndMT) is a biological process where endothelial cells lose their specific characteristics and acquire mesenchymal properties, often contributing to tissue fibrosis and dysfunction.
